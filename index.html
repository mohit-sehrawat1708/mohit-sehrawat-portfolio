AccessLens

an accessibility audit for any URL

Paste a URL and get a scored 0–100 accessibility report. Every issue comes with a severity, the WCAG criterion it breaks and a concrete fix.

  • Colour contrast that handles alpha compositing, CSS variables, gradients and @media rules, and computes a passing colour
  • Missing or poor alt text, keyboard-navigation problems, structure and semantics
  • Runs locally: nothing stored, no external services
